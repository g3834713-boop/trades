How Our Order System Works
1
Daily Order Matching
Every active account is eligible to receive one automated order match per day through our LBS cloud technology system.
2
Standard Commission Rate
You earn a consistent 20% commission on the total order amount. Commission is calculated automatically and added to your account upon successful order completion.
3
LBS Technology Matching
Our advanced Location-Based Services system automatically matches suitable products to your profile, ensuring you receive relevant orders.
4
Time-Sensitive Submission
Once an order is matched to you, you must confirm and submit it within the specified time limit. Failure to submit within the deadline will result in the order being closed and the order amount being frozen from your account.
5
Account Score Impact
Your account score directly reflects your reliability and performance. Late or incomplete submissions reduce your score, which affects your eligibility for future order matches and affects your reputation with merchants. If you cannot complete an order, use the "Stop Order" option to prevent penalties.
6
Withdrawal Requirements
You must complete all active orders and resolve any pending disputes with merchants before you can withdraw your earnings. Ensure all LBS-based tasks are finished to maintain withdrawal eligibility.
