DailyTrade Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DailyTrade (the "Platform") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — name, email address, and phone number provided at registration.
  • Identity verification — where required for withdrawals above the verification threshold, verification details you choose to submit.
  • Task activity — content you submit to complete tasks, including quiz and poll responses and, for Photo Survey, the photos and captions you upload.
  • Financial activity — wallet balance, transaction history, and withdrawal requests.
  • Referral information — referral codes used or shared, and who you refer.
  • Basic usage data — general device and browser information needed to keep the Platform secure and working correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and process the rewards you earn.
  • To review and approve withdrawal and payment requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and duplicate accounts.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To improve and maintain the Platform.

3. Photos and Task Submissions

Photos submitted through the Photo Survey task are uploaded to a third-party media storage provider and used solely to verify task completion. Please do not include other identifiable people in your photos without their consent.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the Platform (such as authentication, database, and media storage providers), or when required by law.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your data safe and limit access to what's necessary.

6. Your Choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below. Some information may be retained where required for financial record-keeping or fraud prevention.

7. Children's Privacy

The Platform involves financial transactions and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to our support team.
