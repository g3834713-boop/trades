# Quick Earn Task Ideas - Ghana Market

## High-Demand Tasks (Low Entry Cost, Quick Turnaround)

### Social Media Tasks
1. **Follow + Like Campaign** - Cost: GHC 0.50-1.00, Earn: GHC 1.50-2.50
   - Follow Instagram/TikTok accounts
   - Like 5+ posts and comment
   - Screenshot proof required
   - Time: 3-5 minutes

2. **Share & Tag Friends** - Cost: GHC 0.75-1.50, Earn: GHC 2.00-3.50
   - Share post to 3+ friends/groups
   - Tag 5+ friends on post
   - Screenshot proof
   - Time: 2-3 minutes

3. **YouTube Video Subscribe** - Cost: GHC 0.50, Earn: GHC 1.50-2.00
   - Subscribe to channel
   - Watch 2+ minutes of video
   - Like and comment
   - Time: 5-10 minutes

### Mobile/App Tasks
4. **Download & Install App** - Cost: GHC 1.00-2.00, Earn: GHC 3.00-5.00
   - Download specific app
   - Install and open
   - Screenshot proof
   - 1 hour hold time (no uninstall)

5. **Mobile App Signup** - Cost: GHC 0.75, Earn: GHC 2.00-2.50
   - Sign up for new app/service
   - Verify email/phone
   - Upload screenshot of dashboard
   - Time: 10-15 minutes

6. **Game Play & Achievement** - Cost: GHC 0.50-1.50, Earn: GHC 2.00-3.50
   - Download mobile game
   - Reach level 5+ or unlock achievement
   - Screenshot proof
   - Time: 15-30 minutes

### Online Survey & Research
7. **Product Review Survey** - Cost: GHC 0.75, Earn: GHC 2.00-3.00
   - Answer 10-15 questions about product
   - Minimum 2 minutes per survey
   - Honest answers only
   - Time: 5-10 minutes

8. **Website Feedback Form** - Cost: GHC 0.50, Earn: GHC 1.50-2.00
   - Visit website
   - Complete feedback form
   - Submit and screenshot
   - Time: 5 minutes

### Writing/Content Tasks
9. **Product Description** - Cost: GHC 2.00-3.00, Earn: GHC 5.00-8.00
   - Write 100-150 word product description
   - Original, SEO-friendly
   - Submit and approve
   - Time: 15-20 minutes

10. **Social Media Caption** - Cost: GHC 1.00-1.50, Earn: GHC 2.50-4.00
    - Write engaging caption for image
    - Include hashtags
    - Minimum 30 words
    - Time: 10 minutes

### Click & Browse Tasks
11. **Website Click Task** - Cost: GHC 0.25, Earn: GHC 0.75-1.00
    - Visit website
    - Stay on page for 30 seconds
    - Screenshot proof
    - Time: 1 minute

12. **Email Signup** - Cost: GHC 0.50, Earn: GHC 1.50-2.00
    - Sign up for newsletter
    - Confirm email
    - Screenshot proof
    - Time: 3-5 minutes

### Data Entry Tasks
13. **Data Copying** - Cost: GHC 1.50-2.00, Earn: GHC 4.00-6.00
    - Copy data from image/PDF to spreadsheet
    - 20-50 entries
    - Accuracy required
    - Time: 20-30 minutes

14. **Product Listing** - Cost: GHC 2.00-3.00, Earn: GHC 5.00-7.00
    - List 10-15 products on platform
    - Include title, description, price
    - Time: 30-45 minutes

### Watch & View Tasks
15. **Video Watch Task** - Cost: GHC 0.50, Earn: GHC 1.25-1.75
    - Watch YouTube/TikTok video
    - Minimum 2 minutes
    - Can't skip ads
    - Time: 2-5 minutes

16. **Website Explore** - Cost: GHC 0.50, Earn: GHC 1.50-2.00
    - Browse website sections
    - Visit at least 3 pages
    - Stay 2+ minutes total
    - Time: 5-10 minutes

## Medium-Difficulty Tasks (Higher Earnings)

### Referral & Recruitment
17. **Referral Signup** - Cost: GHC 0.00, Earn: GHC 3.00-5.00
    - Refer friend to platform
    - Friend must complete 1 task
    - One-time per referral
    - Time: Passive

18. **Group Recruitment** - Cost: GHC 5.00, Earn: GHC 15.00-25.00
    - Recruit 5+ active members to group
    - Each must earn minimum GHC 2.00
    - Weekly payout
    - Time: Ongoing

### Translation Tasks
19. **Text Translation** - Cost: GHC 1.50-2.50, Earn: GHC 4.00-6.00
    - Translate 200-300 words
    - English ↔ Twi/Ga/Hausa
    - Natural, fluent
    - Time: 20-30 minutes

### Photo/Image Tasks
20. **Photo Verification** - Cost: GHC 0.75, Earn: GHC 2.00-2.50
    - Take photo of specific location
    - Verify with GPS metadata
    - Quality check
    - Time: 15-20 minutes

21. **Screenshot Validation** - Cost: GHC 0.50, Earn: GHC 1.50-2.00
    - Take screenshot of task completion
    - Verify timestamp visible
    - Multiple submissions allowed
    - Time: 5 minutes

## Recommended Task Mix for Platform

**Daily Rotation (update tasks every 2-3 days):**
- 20% High-volume low-cost (Tasks 1-3, 11, 15) = get users hooked
- 40% Medium tasks (Tasks 4-6, 8-10, 12) = bread and butter
- 20% Data/Writing tasks (Tasks 13-14, 19) = higher earners
- 10% App download tasks (Task 5) = passive recurring
- 10% Referral tasks (Task 17) = growth

## Ghana-Specific Considerations

✅ **Accept:** 
- MTN Momo, Vodafone Cash payouts
- WhatsApp group recruitment
- Twi/Ga language tasks
- Screenshots (wide adoption)

❌ **Avoid:**
- Tasks requiring international payments
- Gambling/betting tasks
- VPN-required tasks
- Tasks targeting minors

## Commission Suggestion
- **User Cost:** 50% of earning amount
- **Platform Take:** 50% profit (sustainable)
- **Example:** Task costs GHC 1.00, user earns GHC 2.00, platform makes GHC 1.00

This ratio keeps users motivated while ensuring platform sustainability.
